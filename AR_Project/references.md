# 📚 References & Sources

## Academic Sources

1. **Azuma, R. T.** (1997). "A Survey of Augmented Reality." *Presence: Teleoperators and Virtual Environments*, 6(4), 355–385. MIT Press.
   - Foundational paper defining AR and its key characteristics.
   - Used for: Definition of AR, three characteristics of AR systems.

2. **Milgram, P., & Kishino, F.** (1994). "A Taxonomy of Mixed Reality Visual Displays." *IEICE Transactions on Information and Systems*, E77-D(12), 1321–1329.
   - Introduces the Reality-Virtuality Continuum.
   - Used for: AR vs VR comparison, understanding the spectrum between reality and virtuality.

3. **Billinghurst, M., Clark, A., & Lee, G.** (2015). "A Survey of Augmented Reality." *Foundations and Trends in Human-Computer Interaction*, 8(2–3), 73–272.
   - Comprehensive survey of AR technology, applications, and future directions.
   - Used for: Types of AR, application domains, limitations.

4. **Carmigniani, J., et al.** (2011). "Augmented Reality Technologies, Systems and Applications." *Multimedia Tools and Applications*, 51(1), 341–377. Springer.
   - Overview of AR systems and their components.
   - Used for: How AR works (tracking, registration, display).

5. **Wu, H. K., et al.** (2013). "Current Status, Opportunities and Challenges of Augmented Reality in Education." *Computers & Education*, 62, 41–49. Elsevier.
   - Analysis of AR's effectiveness in educational settings.
   - Used for: AR in education applications, learning outcomes.
